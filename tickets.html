Good to Know

When can I buy Early Bird or General tickets?

Early Bird tickets ($8) are sold online from May 12 to May 28, 2027 (one month to two weeks before the festival). General Admission ($15) is sold online from May 29 to June 11. The two are never on sale at the same time. On festival day, tickets are $18 at the gate.

Do kids need a ticket?

No. Children under 12 get in free and don't need a ticket. Their age is verified at the festival entrance.

Are volunteers free?

Yes. Registered volunteers get free entry and don't need a ticket; just check in with staff at the entrance. Not signed up yet? Become a volunteer.

Will alcohol be served?

There is a fenced 21+ Beer Area on site. Please bring a valid ID.

Is the venue accessible?

ADA-compliant restrooms are available, and staff at the First Aid & Info HQ can help with any needs.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email info@mccollege.edu or call (312) 236-9000.
